── 3D Environments ──────────────────────────────────────────

Gwangju_3D asset
  by Gwangju Arts & Culture Humanities Story Platform (광주문화예술인문스토리플랫폼)
  — Gwangju Metropolitan City cultural heritage digital archive project.
    Free 3D assets published on Unity Asset Store and Sketchfab (CC Attribution).
    Scenes used as the outdoor environment:
    • Gungjeon Bakery (06_GungjeonBakery)
    • Dong-A Silk (27_Dong-A_Silk)
    • Jeonbyungwon Tailor Shop (24_JeonbyungwonTailorShop)
  Includes tree/rock/bush prefabs, ground materials, lightmaps, scene profiles.
  https://inmun-story.com / https://sketchfab.com/gwangju_inmunstory
